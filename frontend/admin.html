Commercialisation Plan

Built for Every Railway Network

RailSense AI is a business-to-government (B2G) software-as-a-service suite for railway authorities. It replaces four disconnected tools with one connected system and delivers measurable operational value.

9.1

Value proposition

One platform, five measurable outcomes for the authority, its staff and its passengers.

Better passenger communication

Live, cited, trilingual answers reduce avoidable enquiries and complaints.

Earlier warning

Delay predictions with explanations and equipment-health scores let staff act before a problem spreads.

Faster triage

Incidents are classified and summarised automatically, then verified by a person before the public sees them.

Safer ticketing

Unusual booking behaviour is flagged with plain-language reasons and reviewed by staff.

Trust for government buyers

Signed messages, role-based access, masked identifiers and full audit trails answer the first question any authority asks about data protection.

9.4

Cost structure

The architecture keeps running costs predictable. Facts come from trained models, databases and deterministic services; language models only phrase answers, so usage is bounded, and providers can be swapped through configuration.

Table 18. Cost drivers and their current basis

9.5

Deployment options

Cloud multi-tenant SaaS

Stateless services with managed Supabase and Upstash; fastest to start and easiest to update.

Single server or containers

start.py or docker-compose.yml on the authority’s own infrastructure.

On-premise

For data-residency-sensitive contracts: because every LLM path has a deterministic fallback and retrieval can run on local TF-IDF, the platform can operate with minimal external dependencies.

9.7

Revenue model and sustainability

Revenue is recurring subscription revenue per station, with onboarding fees and add-ons on top. Growth comes from three directions:

More stations per customer

Each additional station adds recurring subscription revenue.

Movement up the tiers

Authorities adopt more agents as they grow, from Starter to Operations to Enterprise.

New customers in adjacent sectors

Metro, bus, tourist and freight operators in the wider transport sector.

Because the agents are separate services with clear interfaces, new capabilities can be sold as additional modules without redesigning the platform.

All prices are indicative planning figures, to be finalised with the customer after a pilot.
