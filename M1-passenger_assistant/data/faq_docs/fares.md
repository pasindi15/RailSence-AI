# Fare Information — Colombo to Kandy Line

## Standard fares (one-way)
- First class (observation/air-conditioned): LKR 1,500
- Second class (reserved): LKR 400
- Third class (unreserved): LKR 150

## Reservation policy
Reservations for first and second class can be made up to 30 days in
advance at any railway station booking counter or through the official
online booking portal. Third class seating is unreserved and first-come,
first-served.

## Concessions
- Students with a valid student ID receive a 50% discount on second and
  third class fares.
- Senior citizens (60+) receive a 25% discount on all classes with valid
  national ID.
- Children under 5 travel free when accompanied by a paying adult; children
  aged 5-12 receive a 50% discount.

## Refunds and cancellations
Tickets cancelled more than 24 hours before departure receive a full
refund minus a small service fee. Cancellations within 24 hours of
departure are non-refundable except in cases of documented medical
emergency.

## Season tickets
Monthly season tickets are available for commuters on regular routes at a
significant discount compared to individual fares. Apply at any major
station ticket counter with proof of address.
