# General Passenger Policies

## Luggage
Passengers may carry up to 40kg of personal luggage free of charge in
second and third class, and up to 60kg in first class. Excess luggage is
charged per kilogram at the station counter. Bicycles are permitted in
designated luggage vans on most routes for a small additional fee.

## Pets
Small pets in carriers are permitted in third class only, at the
discretion of the conductor. Guide dogs and service animals are permitted
in all classes free of charge.

## Delays and disruptions
In the event of a delay exceeding 60 minutes, passengers holding a
reserved ticket for a cancelled or significantly delayed service are
entitled to a full refund or free rebooking on the next available train.
Announcements for delays are made at the station and, where available,
via the RailSense passenger app.

## Reporting an issue
Passengers can report broken equipment (air conditioning, seating,
toilets, etc.) directly through the RailSense chat assistant or at the
station help desk. Reports are forwarded to the maintenance team for
follow-up.

## Lost property
Lost items should be reported to the station master's office at the
destination station or the point of boarding. Items are held for 30 days
before being processed according to railway lost-property policy.

## Accessibility
Priority seating is available in every carriage for elderly passengers,
pregnant passengers, and passengers with disabilities. Wheelchair-
accessible carriages are available on express services — request this
when booking.
