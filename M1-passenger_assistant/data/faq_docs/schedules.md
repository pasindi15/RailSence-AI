# Train Schedules — Colombo to Kandy Line

## Podi Menike (Express)
Departs Colombo Fort at 08:30, arrives Kandy at 11:10.
Stops: Colombo Fort, Peradeniya, Kandy.

## Udarata Menike (Express)
Departs Colombo Fort at 09:45, arrives Kandy at 12:35.
Stops: Colombo Fort, Rambukkana, Kadugannawa, Peradeniya, Kandy.

## Evening Express
Departs Colombo Fort at 14:35, arrives Kandy at 17:20.
Stops: Colombo Fort, Peradeniya, Kandy.

## Night Mail
Departs Colombo Fort at 20:00, arrives Kandy at 23:15.
Stops: Colombo Fort, Rambukkana, Peradeniya, Kandy.

## General schedule notes
Trains run daily including weekends and public holidays, except where noted
during track maintenance windows. Schedules are subject to change during
the Kandy Esala Perahera season (July/August) — check for special notices
during that period. First class, second class, and third class seating are
available on all express services. Reserved seating is recommended for
first and second class, especially on weekends.
