# Passenger Policies (reference data - booking, cancellation and refund rules match the booking system; verify the rest against actual SLR policy before final submission)

## Booking Rules
- Tickets can be reserved up to 30 days before the departure date.
- One booking can include at most 10 seats.
- The National Identity Card (NIC) number of every passenger is required to make a booking.
- Every confirmed booking gets a booking reference in the format RS-XXXXX. Keep it, because it is needed to cancel or ask about the booking.
- If there is a mistake in the passenger details or the travel date, report it within 24 hours of the booking being issued.

## Cancelling a Booking
- Only a confirmed booking can be cancelled, and the cancellation request must be made before the scheduled departure.
- To cancel, tell this assistant "cancel booking RS-XXXXX" and give the reason. The assistant prepares a cancellation request for the passenger to confirm and send.
- Sending a cancellation request does not cancel the booking immediately. The request goes to an administrator for review, and the booking stays confirmed until the administrator approves or rejects it.
- This assistant cannot cancel a booking or pay a refund by itself. A human administrator makes the decision.

## Refunds
- The refund depends on how long before the scheduled departure the cancellation request is made. The amount is a suggestion until the administrator approves it.
- More than 48 hours before departure: 75% of the ticket fare is refunded (25% administrative fee deducted).
- 24 to 48 hours before departure: 50% of the ticket fare is refunded (50% deducted).
- Less than 24 hours before departure, or after departure: no refund (0%).
- Exception - duplicate booking: if the same route and date was booked twice by mistake and the cancellation is submitted within 24 hours of booking, a 100% refund is given once the duplicate is verified.
- Exception - railway service disruption: if the train is cancelled, derails, is delayed by more than 3 hours, or there is an official rail strike, the refund is 100% with no deductions.
- Exception - certified personal emergency: with documented medical hospitalization or a severe emergency, an 80% refund is given after the administrator verifies the documents.
- Exception - change of travel plans for official work or an examination schedule, with more than 48 hours notice: 75% refund.
- Refund amounts are calculated exactly by the booking system. The assistant does not estimate them.

## Reserved Seating
- Seats are assigned in the order bookings are confirmed.
- Tickets and seat assignments cannot be transferred to another person unless an administrator officially re-endorses them.
- Booking the same passenger on the same train and date twice is discouraged. If it happens by mistake, tell the booking authority straight away and quote the duplicate booking reference. An administrator keeps one confirmed booking and approves cancelling the duplicate with a 100% refund.

## Ticket Validity
- Point-to-point tickets are valid only for the date and train specified at time of purchase.
- Season tickets are valid for unlimited travel between the two specified stations for the purchased duration (weekly, monthly, or quarterly).

## Luggage
- Each passenger may carry one piece of luggage up to 25kg free of charge in reserved classes.
- Excess luggage is charged at LKR 5 per kg above the free allowance.
- Bicycles and large sporting equipment require a separate luggage ticket, purchased at the station.

## Delays and Cancellations
- Sri Lanka Railways does not guarantee arrival/departure times shown in the schedule; delays may occur due to weather, signal faults, or track maintenance.
- If the railway cancels a service, or a train is delayed by more than 3 hours, or there is an official rail strike, passengers are entitled to a full 100% refund with no deductions. Send a cancellation request for the booking and give the disruption as the reason.

## Complaints and Issue Reporting
- Faults with train equipment (AC, doors, seating) reported during travel are logged and forwarded to the Maintenance Department for inspection. To report one here, describe the problem (and the train ID if known) and the assistant will log it and give a ticket reference.
- Formal complaints can also be filed at any staffed station counter or via the passenger helpline.
