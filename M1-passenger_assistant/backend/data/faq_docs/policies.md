# Policies (reference data - verify against actual SLR policy before final submission)

## Refunds
- Reserved tickets can be refunded up to 2 hours before scheduled
  departure, with a 10% service charge deducted.
- No refunds are issued within 2 hours of departure or after
  departure.
- Unreserved (3rd class) tickets are non-refundable once issued.

## Luggage
- Each passenger may carry one piece of luggage up to 25kg free of
  charge in reserved classes.
- Excess luggage is charged at LKR 5 per kg above the free allowance.
- Bicycles and large sporting equipment require a separate luggage
  ticket, purchased at the station.

## Reserved Seating
- Reserved seats must be claimed at least 10 minutes before scheduled
  departure. Unclaimed reserved seats may be reassigned to waitlisted
  passengers after this window.
- Reservations can be made up to 30 days in advance and up to 1 hour
  before departure, subject to availability.

## Ticket Validity
- Point-to-point tickets are valid only for the date and train
  specified at time of purchase.
- Season tickets are valid for unlimited travel between the two
  specified stations for the purchased duration (weekly, monthly, or
  quarterly).

## Delays and Cancellations
- Sri Lanka Railways does not guarantee arrival/departure times shown
  in the schedule; delays may occur due to weather, signal faults, or
  track maintenance.
- In the event of a cancelled service, passengers may use their ticket
  on the next available train on the same route at no extra charge.

## Complaints and Issue Reporting
- Faults with train equipment (AC, doors, seating) reported during
  travel are logged and forwarded to the Maintenance Department for
  inspection.
- Formal complaints can also be filed at any staffed station counter
  or via the passenger helpline.
