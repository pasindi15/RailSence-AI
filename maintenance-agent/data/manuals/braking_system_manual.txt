RAILSENSE ROLLING STOCK MAINTENANCE MANUAL
Component: Braking System
Document Reference: RSM-BRAKE-4.2
Revision: 3.1 | Effective Date: 2025-01-01

================================================================================
SECTION 1 — OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The braking system is a safety-critical assembly responsible for controlled
deceleration and stopping of the rolling stock. It comprises hydraulic disc
brakes, brake calipers, brake pads (part number BR-4021), hydraulic actuators
(part number BR-7710), and the emergency brake override unit (part number
BR-8801). All components must be maintained in strict accordance with this
manual to ensure passenger safety and regulatory compliance.

================================================================================
SECTION 2 — INSPECTION INTERVALS
================================================================================

2.1 Routine Inspection
Perform a full braking system inspection every 90 days or every 15,000 km,
whichever occurs first. Record all readings in the maintenance log.

2.2 Interim Visual Check
A visual check of brake pad wear indicators and hydraulic line integrity must
be performed every 30 days or 5,000 km.

2.3 Emergency Brake Test
A full emergency brake application test must be conducted every 180 days.
Document the stopping distance and compare against the baseline in Table 3.1.

================================================================================
SECTION 3 — INSPECTION PROCEDURES
================================================================================

3.1 Brake Pad Inspection (Part BR-4021)
Measure brake pad thickness using a calibrated pad-thickness gauge.
- Acceptable: 12 mm or above — no action required.
- Advisory (AMBER): 10–12 mm — schedule replacement within 30 days.
- Critical (RED): below 10 mm — replace immediately before returning to service.
Uneven wear, glazing, or surface cracking are signs of caliper misalignment.
Inspect caliper mounting bolts (torque to 85 Nm) if uneven wear is detected.

3.2 Hydraulic Pressure Check
Connect a calibrated pressure gauge to test port HYD-TP1.
- Normal operating pressure: 3.5 to 5.5 bar.
- Low pressure warning (AMBER): 1.5 to 3.5 bar — inspect for hydraulic leaks.
- Critical low pressure (RED): below 1.5 bar — remove from service immediately.
- Over-pressure (RED): above 6.0 bar — inspect pressure relief valve PRV-2200.
Oil pressure below 1.5 bar typically indicates a hydraulic fluid leak or a
failed hydraulic pump. Do not operate the vehicle until the fault is resolved.

3.3 Temperature Monitoring
Brake disc surface temperature should be measured immediately after a standard
braking cycle using an infrared thermometer.
- Normal: below 80°C.
- Advisory (AMBER): 80–95°C — increase inspection frequency to weekly.
- Critical (RED): above 95°C — investigate immediately. Likely causes include
  dragging brakes, contaminated discs, or a seized caliper. Continued operation
  above 95°C risks disc warping and total braking failure.

3.4 Vibration During Braking
Vibration level is measured on the caliper bracket using an accelerometer.
- Normal: 0 to 3 mm/s² — acceptable.
- Advisory (AMBER): 3 to 5 mm/s² — inspect caliper alignment and disc surface.
- Critical (RED): above 5 mm/s² — indicates severe disc warping, worn pad
  backing plate, or loose caliper mounting. Remove from service immediately.

================================================================================
SECTION 4 — FAULT DIAGNOSIS
================================================================================

4.1 Symptom: Excessive Vibration During Braking
Cause: Warped brake disc, loose caliper, worn backing plate (BR-4021 variant B).
Action: Measure disc runout — maximum allowable is 0.15 mm. Replace disc
part number BR-DISC-9900 if runout exceeds limit. Torque caliper to 85 Nm.

4.2 Symptom: High Operating Temperature (above 95°C)
Cause: Dragging brakes, contaminated disc, seized caliper piston.
Action: Release brakes manually and check caliper piston retraction. Flush
hydraulic fluid if contaminated. Replace caliper if piston travel is restricted.

4.3 Symptom: Oil Pressure Drop Below 1.5 Bar
Cause: Hydraulic fluid leak, failed pump, cracked hydraulic line.
Action: Inspect all hydraulic lines for leaks. Check fluid reservoir level.
Replace hydraulic pump assembly HP-5500 if pressure cannot be restored.

4.4 Symptom: Spongy Brake Pedal Feel
Cause: Air in hydraulic lines.
Action: Bleed hydraulic system at bleed nipple BN-1100. Refill with approved
hydraulic fluid to the MAX line on reservoir. Repeat pressure check.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 — SERVICE PROCEDURES
================================================================================

5.1 Brake Pad Replacement (BR-4021)
1. Raise and secure the bogie.
2. Remove caliper mounting bolts (2x M14, torque 85 Nm on reassembly).
3. Slide out worn pads. Do not allow caliper to hang by the hydraulic line.
4. Retract caliper piston using piston retraction tool RT-220.
5. Insert new pads — minimum new pad thickness is 18 mm.
6. Reinstall caliper. Torque bolts to 85 Nm.
7. Apply brakes 5–10 times to bed in new pads before returning to service.

5.2 Hydraulic Fluid Change
Hydraulic fluid must be replaced every 180 days regardless of condition.
Use only approved fluid grade HYD-46 or equivalent. Mixing fluid grades will
cause seal degradation and premature pump failure.

5.3 Caliper Overhaul
Full caliper overhaul is required every 360 days or at the third brake pad
replacement, whichever comes first. Use caliper rebuild kit CRK-3300.

================================================================================
SECTION 7 — SAFETY WARNINGS
================================================================================

WARNING: Never operate rolling stock with brake system pressure below 1.5 bar.
WARNING: Brake disc temperature above 95°C indicates an active fault condition.
WARNING: Vibration levels above 5 mm/s² require immediate service withdrawal.
WARNING: Always chock wheels before beginning brake system maintenance.
WARNING: Hydraulic fluid is flammable. Keep away from heat sources.
