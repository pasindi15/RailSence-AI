RAILSENSE ROLLING STOCK MAINTENANCE MANUAL
Component: Air Compressor
Document Reference: RSM-COMP-5.7
Revision: 2.2 | Effective Date: 2025-01-01

================================================================================
SECTION 1 — OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The air compressor provides compressed air for the braking system, door
actuation, suspension levelling, horn, and auxiliary pneumatic systems.
Each locomotive is fitted with a twin-cylinder reciprocating compressor
(part number CP-MAIN-1100) driven by the auxiliary power supply. Key
sub-components include the air dryer unit (CP-DRYER-2200), unloader valve
(CP-UNLDR-3300), safety relief valve (CP-SRV-4400), oil separator
(CP-OILSEP-5500), compressor oil pump (CP-PUMP-6600), and delivery check
valve (CP-CHK-7700).

Compressor failure results in loss of braking air pressure and is a
safety-critical service-affecting fault.

================================================================================
SECTION 2 — INSPECTION INTERVALS
================================================================================

2.1 Routine Inspection
Full compressor inspection every 90 days or 15,000 km, whichever is first.

2.2 Oil Level and Quality Check
Check compressor oil level and clarity every 30 days.
Oil change interval: every 90 days regardless of appearance.

2.3 Air Dryer Inspection
Inspect air dryer desiccant cartridge (CP-DRYER-2200) every 90 days.
Replace desiccant if moisture saturation indicator has turned pink.

2.4 Belt / Drive Inspection (if belt-driven)
Inspect drive belt tension and condition every 45 days.

================================================================================
SECTION 3 — INSPECTION PROCEDURES
================================================================================

3.1 Delivery Pressure Check
Connect a calibrated pressure gauge to the main reservoir delivery port.
The compressor must achieve and maintain the following pressures:
- Cut-in pressure: 6.5 bar (compressor starts).
- Cut-out pressure: 8.5 bar (compressor stops via unloader valve).
- Minimum system pressure for brake release: 4.5 bar.
- Low pressure alarm threshold (AMBER): below 3.5 bar.
- Critical low pressure (RED): below 1.5 bar — loss of braking air.
  Remove from service immediately if main reservoir pressure falls below
  1.5 bar. The vehicle cannot safely operate without adequate brake pressure.

3.2 Operating Temperature
Compressor head temperature is measured with an IR thermometer on the
cylinder head immediately after a loading cycle.
- Normal: 60–85°C.
- Advisory (AMBER): 85–95°C — check oil level and cooling air path.
- Critical (RED): above 95°C — shut down compressor immediately. Over-
  temperature indicates oil starvation, blocked cooling fins, or internal
  mechanical fault. Continued operation risks piston seizure and cylinder
  scoring.

3.3 Vibration Measurement
Measure vibration on the compressor mounting frame.
- Normal: 0 to 3 mm/s² — serviceable.
- Advisory (AMBER): 3 to 5 mm/s² — inspect mounting bolts and check for
  loose drive coupling. Inspect cylinder valve plates (CP-VALVE-8800).
- Critical (RED): above 5 mm/s² — indicates a broken valve plate, piston
  knock, or bearing failure (CP-BRG-9900). Remove from service immediately.

3.4 Oil Pressure Check (Lubricated Models)
Compressor internal oil pressure must be verified during operation.
- Normal oil pressure: 2.0 to 5.0 bar.
- Low oil pressure (AMBER): 1.5 to 2.0 bar — top up oil and monitor.
- Critical low (RED): below 1.5 bar — stop compressor. Likely oil pump
  failure (CP-PUMP-6600) or severe oil loss. Do not restart until fault resolved.
- Over-pressure (RED): above 6.0 bar — inspect pressure relief valve (CP-SRV-4400).

3.5 Oil Quality and Level Check
- Use the sight glass to verify oil is between MIN and MAX marks.
- Acceptable: clear or light amber colour.
- Discard if: milky (water contamination), dark brown (overheating), or
  metallic particles visible (internal wear). Flush and refill with grade
  CP-OIL-32 only.

3.6 Air Dryer Check (CP-DRYER-2200)
Moisture in the air system causes rust, frozen valves, and ice in brake
pipework in cold conditions. Replace desiccant when indicator turns pink.
Inspect the purge valve for correct operation — it must exhaust moisture
at each cut-out cycle (audible "pfft" every 2–3 minutes during normal operation).

================================================================================
SECTION 4 — FAULT DIAGNOSIS
================================================================================

4.1 Symptom: Low System Air Pressure (below 1.5 bar)
Cause 1: Compressor not building pressure — worn piston rings.
Action: Conduct a cylinder compression test. If below 7 bar, replace rings.
Cause 2: Large air leak in the main reservoir or pipework.
Action: Apply soapy water to all joints. Fix all identified leaks.
Cause 3: Unloader valve stuck open.
Action: Inspect and replace unloader valve CP-UNLDR-3300.

4.2 Symptom: Overheating (above 95°C)
Cause 1: Oil starvation — oil level below MIN.
Action: Top up with CP-OIL-32. Investigate source of oil loss.
Cause 2: Blocked cooling fins.
Action: Clean fins with compressed air. Do not use water jet.
Cause 3: Continuous loading — demand exceeding compressor capacity.
Action: Check for excessive air leaks causing the compressor to run continuously.

4.3 Symptom: Vibration Above 5 mm/s²
Cause 1: Broken cylinder valve plate (CP-VALVE-8800).
Action: Replace valve plate set.
Cause 2: Worn connecting rod bearing.
Action: Workshop overhaul required. Replace bearing CP-BRG-9900.

4.4 Symptom: Oil in Delivered Air
Cause: Failed oil separator (CP-OILSEP-5500).
Action: Replace oil separator cartridge. Check piston rings.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 — SERVICE PROCEDURES
================================================================================

5.1 Oil Change
1. Run compressor for 5 minutes to warm oil.
2. Drain via drain plug at base of crankcase.
3. Fill with approved grade CP-OIL-32 to MAX line.
4. Run for 2 minutes and recheck level.

5.2 Valve Plate Replacement (CP-VALVE-8800)
1. Depressurise system completely.
2. Remove cylinder head (8x M10 bolts, 55 Nm on reassembly).
3. Remove old valve plate assembly.
4. Clean mating surfaces.
5. Install new valve plate with new cylinder head gasket CP-GASK-1200.
6. Torque cylinder head bolts in cross pattern to 55 Nm.
7. Run and verify delivery pressure reaches cut-out pressure.

5.3 Full Compressor Overhaul (Every 720 Days)
Full disassembly: replace piston rings, valve plates, bearings, oil seals,
and air dryer desiccant. Clean all internal surfaces. Reassemble and test.

================================================================================
SECTION 7 — SAFETY WARNINGS
================================================================================

WARNING: Never operate with system pressure below 1.5 bar — loss of braking.
WARNING: Compressor temperature above 95°C is an immediate shutdown condition.
WARNING: Vibration above 5 mm/s² indicates mechanical failure — do not run.
WARNING: Always fully depressurise before opening any compressor covers.
WARNING: Relief valve must never be bypassed or set above factory pressure.
