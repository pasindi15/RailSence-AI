RAILSENSE ROLLING STOCK MAINTENANCE MANUAL
Component: Traction Motor
Document Reference: RSM-TRMOT-6.1
Revision: 2.4 | Effective Date: 2025-01-01

================================================================================
SECTION 1 — OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The traction motor converts electrical energy into mechanical torque to drive
the axles. Each locomotive is fitted with two traction motors (part number
TM-3301) per bogie, coupled to the axle via a hollow shaft flexible drive.
Supporting components include the motor cooling blower (TM-4402), motor
controller unit (TM-5503), and temperature sensors (TM-TSENS-6600).

Traction motors are among the highest-wear components in the rolling stock
fleet. Adherence to inspection intervals is mandatory to prevent in-service
failure, which constitutes a service-affecting incident.

================================================================================
SECTION 2 — INSPECTION INTERVALS
================================================================================

2.1 Routine Inspection
Full traction motor inspection every 90 days or 20,000 km, whichever is first.

2.2 Carbon Brush Inspection
Inspect carbon brushes (part TM-BRUSH-7700) every 45 days. Measure brush
length and contact surface condition.

2.3 Insulation Resistance Test
Perform megohmmeter insulation test every 90 days before service starts.
Minimum acceptable insulation resistance: 1 MΩ at 500 V DC.

2.4 Post-Incident Inspection
A full motor inspection is mandatory within 24 hours of any speed overshoot,
electrical fault, or emergency brake application at speeds above 80 km/h.

================================================================================
SECTION 3 — INSPECTION PROCEDURES
================================================================================

3.1 Winding Temperature Monitoring
Winding temperature is monitored continuously via sensor TM-TSENS-6600.
- Normal: 60–80°C under full load.
- Advisory (AMBER): 80–95°C — reduce load, increase cooling airflow check.
- Critical (RED): above 95°C — shut down traction motor immediately. Continued
  operation above 95°C will cause winding insulation breakdown, leading to
  inter-turn short circuits and irreparable winding damage.
After any overtemperature event, the insulation resistance must be re-tested
before returning the motor to service.

3.2 Vibration Level Measurement
Measure vibration at the motor mounting foot using a calibrated accelerometer.
- Normal: 0 to 3 mm/s² — serviceable.
- Advisory (AMBER): 3 to 5 mm/s² — inspect motor bearings and coupling.
- Critical (RED): above 5 mm/s² — remove from service. Likely cause is a
  failed bearing (TM-BRG-8800 or TM-BRG-8801), a damaged armature, or a
  loose mounting bolt. Do not continue operation — bearing seizure risk.

3.3 Carbon Brush Inspection (TM-BRUSH-7700)
Minimum acceptable brush length: 25 mm.
Replace when brush length falls below 25 mm or if the contact surface shows
chipping, cracking, or uneven wear.
Brushes worn below 15 mm will arc excessively, scoring the commutator surface
(part TM-COMM-9900) and causing catastrophic winding damage.

3.4 Cooling Airflow Check
The cooling blower (TM-4402) must deliver a minimum of 0.8 m³/s airflow.
Measure airflow at the blower outlet with an anemometer.
- Reduced airflow is the primary cause of overtemperature events.
- Clean or replace blower filter element TM-FILT-1100 every 90 days.

3.5 Oil / Grease Check (Hollow Shaft Drive)
The hollow shaft drive requires lubrication with grease grade G-220.
Check grease level every 90 days. Replenish if the sight glass is below
the MIN line. Over-greasing causes overheating — do not exceed MAX line.

================================================================================
SECTION 4 — FAULT DIAGNOSIS
================================================================================

4.1 Symptom: Motor Temperature Above 95°C
Cause 1: Blocked cooling blower filter (TM-FILT-1100).
Action: Clean or replace filter. Verify airflow reaches 0.8 m³/s.
Cause 2: Overloaded operation on steep gradient.
Action: Check load profile. Limit gradient operations during peak hours.
Cause 3: Faulty temperature sensor reporting false readings.
Action: Cross-check with handheld IR thermometer.

4.2 Symptom: Vibration Above 5 mm/s²
Cause 1: Worn or seized motor bearing (TM-BRG-8800).
Action: Replace bearing immediately. Check shaft for scoring.
Cause 2: Motor mounting bolt loose.
Action: Inspect all four mounting bolts. Torque to 120 Nm.
Cause 3: Armature imbalance due to carbon deposit build-up.
Action: Clean commutator with approved solvent. Balance armature if needed.

4.3 Symptom: Low Insulation Resistance (below 1 MΩ)
Cause: Moisture ingress or insulation degradation.
Action: Dry motor in oven at 80°C for 6 hours. Retest. If still below 1 MΩ
send for rewind. Do not return to service with wet insulation.

4.4 Symptom: Excessive Arcing at Commutator
Cause: Worn brushes (below 25 mm) or scored commutator.
Action: Replace brushes TM-BRUSH-7700. Skim commutator TM-COMM-9900 if
surface runout exceeds 0.05 mm.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 — SERVICE PROCEDURES
================================================================================

5.1 Carbon Brush Replacement (TM-BRUSH-7700)
1. Isolate traction motor from supply. Lock out / tag out.
2. Remove brush holder cover (4x M10 bolts).
3. Lift brush spring and slide out worn brush.
4. Insert new brush. Ensure correct grade — only use approved TM-BRUSH-7700.
5. Bed in new brushes: operate motor at 50% load for 30 minutes before
   returning to full-load service.

5.2 Bearing Replacement (TM-BRG-8800)
1. Remove motor from bogie frame (requires bogie workshop access).
2. Press out old bearing using bearing press BP-440.
3. Heat new bearing to 80°C before pressing onto shaft.
4. Reassemble and perform vibration check before reinstalling motor.

5.3 Full Motor Service (Every 360 Days)
Full disassembly, cleaning, insulation test, commutator skim, brush and
bearing replacement, and regreasing. Must be performed in a workshop
environment free of moisture and contamination.

================================================================================
SECTION 7 — SAFETY WARNINGS
================================================================================

WARNING: Always isolate and lock out power before opening motor covers.
WARNING: Traction motor temperature above 95°C is a mandatory shutdown condition.
WARNING: Vibration above 5 mm/s² indicates bearing or armature failure risk.
WARNING: Never operate with insulation resistance below 1 MΩ — electrocution risk.
WARNING: Carbon dust from worn brushes is conductive — clean regularly.
