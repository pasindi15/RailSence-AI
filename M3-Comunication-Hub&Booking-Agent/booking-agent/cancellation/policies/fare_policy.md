# DEMO / ACADEMIC RAILWAY POLICY: FARE POLICY
Document ID: POL-FARE-004
Status: ACTIVE (DEMO / ACADEMIC SPECIFICATION)

## Article 1: Fare Structure and Base Rates
1.1 Standard route fares between Colombo and Kandy are calibrated as:
    - First Class: Rs. 2,500.00 per passenger seat.
    - Second Class: Rs. 1,200.00 per passenger seat.
1.2 Total ticket fare is deterministically computed as:
    Total Fare = Base Fare per Seat * Passenger Count.

## Article 2: Non-Refundable Surcharges
2.1 Routine seat reservation processing charges are bundled into the gross fare.
2.2 In standard voluntary cancellations, processing and clerk deductions are computed as percentages of the gross fare as specified in the Refund Policy.
