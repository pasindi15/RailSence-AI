# DEMO / ACADEMIC RAILWAY POLICY: RESERVATION RULES
Document ID: POL-RES-005
Status: ACTIVE (DEMO / ACADEMIC SPECIFICATION)

## Article 1: Seat Assignment and Coach Integrity
1.1 Seat allocation is performed sequentially at time of booking confirmation.
1.2 Tickets and seat assignments are strictly non-transferable between different individuals without official administrative re-endorsement.

## Article 2: Duplicate Reservation Prevention
2.1 Multiple bookings for the identical passenger, train, and date are strongly discouraged by the booking agent.
2.2 When duplicate reservations occur due to system lag or accidental passenger submission, the passenger must notify the booking authority immediately quoting the duplicate booking reference.
2.3 The administrator reviews duplicate booking evidence and ensures only one confirmed reservation is retained, approving cancellation of the duplicate with 100% refund.
