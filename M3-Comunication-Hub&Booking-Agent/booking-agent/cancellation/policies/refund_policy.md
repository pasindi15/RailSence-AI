# DEMO / ACADEMIC RAILWAY POLICY: REFUND POLICY
Document ID: POL-REF-003
Status: ACTIVE (DEMO / ACADEMIC SPECIFICATION)

## Article 1: Schedule-Based Refund Calculations
1.1 Tier 1 (>48 Hours): For voluntary cancellations lodged more than 48 hours prior to train departure, a 75% refund of the total ticket fare shall be granted, with a 25% administrative clerk fee deduction.
1.2 Tier 2 (24 to 48 Hours): For voluntary cancellations lodged between 24 and 48 hours prior to train departure, a 50% refund of the total ticket fare shall be granted, with a 50% cancellation deduction.
1.3 Tier 3 (<24 Hours): Voluntary cancellations lodged less than 24 hours prior to scheduled departure or post-departure are non-refundable (0% refund).

## Article 2: Special Condition Exceptions
2.1 Duplicate Bookings: Where a passenger accidentally books two identical tickets for the same route and travel date, and submits cancellation within 24 hours of booking, a 100% full refund shall be granted upon verification of the duplicate record.
2.2 Railway Service Disruption: In the event of train cancellation, derailment, significant delay exceeding three (3) hours, or official rail strike, passengers are entitled to an immediate 100% full refund with zero deductions.
2.3 Certified Personal Emergency: Where cancellation arises from documented medical hospitalization or severe emergency, the passenger is eligible for an 80% compassionate refund upon administrator verification of supporting documentation.
2.4 Schedule Change by Passenger: When travel plans change due to official work or examination schedule changes with advance notice (>48 hours), standard Tier 1 (75%) refund applies.

## Article 3: Deterministic Financial Precision
3.1 All refund figures must be computed with exact decimal precision (two decimal places) using banker's or deterministic arithmetic.
3.2 Rounding errors or non-deterministic estimations generated by generative models must not determine the authoritative refund amount.
