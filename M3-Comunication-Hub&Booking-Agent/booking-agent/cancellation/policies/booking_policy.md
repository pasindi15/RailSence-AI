# DEMO / ACADEMIC RAILWAY POLICY: BOOKING POLICY
Document ID: POL-BOOK-001
Status: ACTIVE (DEMO / ACADEMIC SPECIFICATION)

## Article 1: Ticket Purchase and Advance Reservations
1.1 Tickets may be reserved up to thirty (30) days in advance of the departure date.
1.2 Each reservation must be issued with a system-unique booking reference formatted as RS-XXXXX.
1.3 Maximum reservation capacity per passenger transaction is capped at ten (10) seats across all coach classes.

## Article 2: Passenger Verification and Identification
2.1 All reservations must record a valid passenger identification number (National ID or Passport number).
2.2 The primary passenger or authorized contact person is responsible for ensuring route and travel date accuracy prior to confirmation.
2.3 Any mistake in passenger details or date should be reported immediately within twenty-four (24) hours of booking issuance.
