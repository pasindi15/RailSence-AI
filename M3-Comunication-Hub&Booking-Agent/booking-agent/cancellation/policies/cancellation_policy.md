# DEMO / ACADEMIC RAILWAY POLICY: CANCELLATION POLICY
Document ID: POL-CANC-002
Status: ACTIVE (DEMO / ACADEMIC SPECIFICATION)

## Article 1: Right of Cancellation
1.1 A passenger holding a confirmed reservation (status CONFIRMED) has the right to initiate a cancellation request prior to the scheduled train departure time.
1.2 Requests for cancellation must state the booking reference (RS-XXXXX) and the original passenger reason for cancellation.
1.3 Once a cancellation request is lodged, it transitions to PENDING_ADMIN_REVIEW status. The underlying booking remains CONFIRMED until formal administrator adjudication.

## Article 2: Cancellation Time Windows and Notice Requirements
2.1 Tier A (Early Notice): Requests submitted more than 48 hours prior to scheduled train departure qualify for standard refund consideration.
2.2 Tier B (Standard Notice): Requests submitted between 24 and 48 hours prior to scheduled departure qualify for partial refund consideration.
2.3 Tier C (Late Notice): Requests submitted less than 24 hours prior to scheduled departure are ineligible for standard voluntary refund, except under certified emergency or service disruption clauses.

## Article 3: Grounded Adjudication and Administrative Review
3.1 No automated AI system or algorithmic pipeline possesses the authority to unilaterally cancel a confirmed passenger booking or finalize refund payouts.
3.2 An authorized human administrator must evaluate each cancellation case, examine policy evidence, and execute either an APPROVE or REJECT decision.
